# Daily challenge policy

The daily puzzle should reward returning with a fresh perspective. The editorial ambition is a puzzle someone can think about across a few hours; this is not a guaranteed or measured solve-time claim. Familiarity, hints and individual experience will change solve times substantially.

New daily rebuses require a target of at least 7, a composed board, and reviewed evidence of at least two distinct, necessary, interacting inferences. Direct picture/word addition, exposed answers and plain arithmetic are rejected even with an inflated score. Existing published dates are returned before the new admission checks, preserving attempts and completions.

AI evidence comes from the existing independent screenshot reviewers at every responsive profile. A candidate without qualifying evidence is skipped so another finalist can be tried. Publication checks the same policy again. Community approval alone no longer qualifies a puzzle for the daily; community play remains available.

The curated fallback contains thirty layered boards with three progressive hints each. Selection retains permanent answer uniqueness and structural novelty checks. Weekly mechanism quotas may be waived only under the existing availability policy; answer reuse and structural duplicates are never waived. The previous picture-addition library is retained as legacy data and is no longer the daily default.

Reasoning evidence is internal solution material. Public puzzle metadata uses an allowlist; reasoning, answer seeds and review notes must never be returned to unsolved players.

Before claiming a typical hours-long challenge, collect blind human playtest results across familiarity levels, hint usage, completion and abandonment. Keep active solving time separate from elapsed time between visits. Automated tests and model review establish policy enforcement and basic fairness, not human difficulty calibration.
