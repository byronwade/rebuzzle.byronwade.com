# AI puzzle craft release — 2026-10-26

Daily generation now designs a mechanism before composing the authoritative board. One bounded structured planning call proposes native layers, visible deductions, a tempting wrong reading, its correcting cue, and three progressive hints. The answer is fixed. The host validates actual catalog assets and supported primitives, then locks the approved layout, coordinates, explanation, and hints. The existing one-repair lane can redesign a failed mechanism.

Seed cues are inspiration in this path, not mandatory literal labels. Legacy generation retains its exact cue contract. Single-candidate daily requests retain planning without increasing the requested candidate count. The default planning model is configurable with `REBUZZLE_MECHANISM_MODEL`; other gameplay models are unchanged.

Critique receives the structured board, and authored hints are preserved rather than padded with generic text. Native text boards no longer need a decorative pictogram. Coincident free-layout text is rejected before rendering; all candidates still face independent rendered recognition, blind solving, editorial reasoning, novelty, and daily admission checks.

## Avoid repeating known early failures

A 48-hour preference keyed by answer, intended technique, and planner version favors other compatible seeds after explicit score/difficulty rejection. Failed pairs remain eligible if alternatives are exhausted. Low-tier and disabled-planner requests are unchanged. Database failure is soft. Asset, missing-evidence, novelty, provider, and unclassified rendered failures do not update this preference. This is intentionally not comprehensive outcome learning.

A production-bank selection smoke check confirmed that a rejected `history repeating itself` pair yields `growing concern` for triple-layer composition and `catch-22` for recursive wordplay when those alternatives are available.

## Live evidence and limits

Small diagnostic trials found and fixed provider `oneOf` incompatibility, overlapping text, literal seed constraints, and lost rejection artifacts. Earlier end-to-end candidates calibrated at difficulty 4–5 and were rejected. A live structured planning response was obtained and rendered at compact, mobile, and desktop sizes; this demonstrated the mechanism path, not publication quality.

Additional trials found reviewed heart/clock icons being rejected by a generated-icon shape-count heuristic before blind recognition. Reviewed catalog and human-approved registry assets now rely on their integrity checks and independent pixel recognition. The shape-count heuristic remains for generated/unapproved assets; vote and confidence thresholds are unchanged.

No benchmark candidate is published as a daily. These small fixture-archive trials do not establish a statistically measured quality uplift or hours-long human solve times. Existing published dates remain unchanged; the reviewed demanding reserve remains available when AI candidates fail.

## Diagnostic tooling

The live benchmark accepts an explicit daily target, records complete available accepted/rejected candidates and daily blockers, and labels small runs diagnostic. Diagnostic mode suppresses training capture, Elo writes, seed-failure writes, and asset quarantine. Research cache is in-process only. Errors redact configured credentials before truncation.

Final checks: 169 Jest suites / 972 tests passed, TypeScript passed, lint exited successfully with 10 pre-existing warnings and one deprecation notice. Independent code review found no remaining actionable issue after fixes. Live heart and clock recognition passed at both 44px and 72px (98% and 95% confidence respectively).

The final two-candidate target-8 diagnostic rejected both candidates: one violated the component budget; the other calibrated at difficulty 5. Zero accepted puzzles in this small run is reported explicitly. This release improves mechanism freedom, asset reliability, hint preservation, and rejection diagnostics; it does not claim demonstrated end-to-end yield or difficulty uplift. Production still falls back to the reviewed reserve when generation fails.
