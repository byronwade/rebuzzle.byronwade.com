# Native Rebuzzle: enjoyment and revenue evidence

Objective: an exceptionally fun single-puzzle-a-day iOS game that players voluntarily return to and that earns sustainable revenue.

Current product: one daily scored puzzle, Eve feedback/hints, spoiler-free sharing, local XP/levels/badges/weekly goals, countdown, opt-in daily reminders, widgets and Siri entry. Optional Plus is a one-time non-consumable for completed-puzzle journal, notes and insights. Price displayed in-app always comes from StoreKit. Current configured US baseline was $9.99; verify in App Store Connect before changing pricing.

Return-loop presentation (October 27, 2026): the solved-result viewport now shows the live next-puzzle countdown and an optional reminder shortcut alongside the share action. The app attempts the daily refresh at the server-provided deadline; if that refresh has not produced the new puzzle, the result offers a manual refresh action. UI tests verify the countdown, reminder control, streak visibility, accessibility text size, and expired-result refresh path. This confirms the interaction ships in the code; it does not prove increased return play. Evaluate it against the existing next-day open and confirmed-finish measures after a production cohort is available.

Extended daily solving (October 27, 2026): the first three guesses remain performance-scored; after three misses, players can keep solving without a score advantage or reveal the answer. A correct continuation earns the normal server-owned daily completion, streak, and Journey progress while its performance score stays locked. The native and web daily clients restore the authoritative session, and an expired run can be reviewed from a dismissible card that leaves today's puzzle available. Legacy sessions remain on their original three-guess rules; community play is unchanged. API, iOS Core, and client UI checks establish implementation behavior, not that puzzles are more enjoyable or that players return more often. Measure solve-after-lock rate, reveal rate, day-two return, and player feedback before making an outcome claim.

Evidence must distinguish implementation from outcomes. Native tests and TestFlight availability prove delivery, not enjoyment, retention, conversion or profitability. There are no measured improvements to those outcomes in this scorecard yet.

Measurement implementation (October 27–28, 2026; production not verified): iOS can report daily opens by entry source, Plus offer views, successful reminder scheduling, an optional four-choice rating after the daily result, and share started / iOS-reported share activity completed. The Plus impression event fires when the dedicated Plus screen opens and when at least half of the contextual Plus offer is visible after a completed free archive sample; the summary counts unique accounts in its reporting window and same-day offer-to-purchase overlap separately. The web game reports a share-attributed open after session restoration. The server records the first accepted daily guess and terminal outcome. A changed rating replaces the previous one for that account and local puzzle day. An admin-only 30-day summary reports opened and finished account-days, finish/open ratio, source counts (including share-attributed opens), share-started and share-completed account-days, share-open days that also have a first guess or finish, accounts returning on multiple puzzle days, Plus offer viewers, accounts with scheduled reminders, and aggregate rating counts. Counts are deduplicated account-days, not raw sends or taps. It exposes aggregates only. Share-open → guess/finish metrics are same-account, same-day overlaps; they do not identify a recipient or prove a distinct person opened the link. An iOS activity completion does not establish delivery or a recipient opening the link. Rating replies are self-selected and directional editorial input, not objective difficulty or enjoyment measurements. Funnel events have a 90-day TTL configured in source and are removed during account deletion; confirm the live production index and deployed event volume before treating this as an operating baseline. Plus offer views are not purchase conversions; use App Store Connect sales for verified purchases. No retention, enjoyment, conversion, or profit outcome has yet been measured.

Puzzle-quality reporting (October 28, 2026; source only, production not verified): the admin summary now includes the latest 30 active official puzzle dates with server-recorded player counts by actual guess count (1, 2, 3, 4+) and outcome (solved, revealed, expired, ran out, in progress). It groups account identity inside Mongo and returns date-level aggregates only. `puzzleAttempts` is shared across platforms, so these counts are all-platform and do not isolate iOS. Synthetic reveal/expiry rows are excluded from guess counts. The source adds a `puzzleDate` index; create it through the normal database index migration before deploying. Existing timing fields do not measure time from seeing a puzzle to finishing it, so this view does not claim hours spent, objective difficulty, or enjoyment. The admin endpoint remains inaccessible from this session, and no player results were read.

Daily quality feedback by puzzle date (October 28, 2026; source only, production not verified): each active puzzle-date row also includes aggregate counts for the latest stored `too_easy`, `just_right`, `tough_but_fair`, and `unclear` rating per account/day. The native result sends its server-provided puzzle date with the existing rating event; the API accepts it only when it matches the stable server-derived local day, and stores only the server-owned day key. The rating control is hidden after rollover or when the result no longer matches the current daily. The dashboard labels these as optional, self-selected native-app responses and displays them separately from all-platform gameplay outcomes. The two samples are not matched, and rating counts do not establish representative difficulty or enjoyment. Dates without ratings show zeroes; feedback-only dates do not create gameplay rows. This adds no event type, retained event field, prompt, index, or migration. The live admin endpoint remains inaccessible from this session, so production coverage and counts are unverified.

Elapsed-to-solve signal (October 28, 2026; source only, production not verified): the authenticated session restore path records one server-timestamped start for the current official daily and joins it to that account's later server-recorded correct solve on the same local puzzle date. Both clients request session restoration during initial daily setup, including first-time empty sessions; this approximates presentation with a small request delay and does not prove visual attention. MongoDB returns only a date-level median, and the dashboard withholds the value below five qualifying solves. This measures wall-clock elapsed time from the first recorded restore, includes idle/background time, and does not measure active thinking time. If analytics storage is unavailable at first restore, a later retry can shorten the interval. It supports difficulty calibration only after enough real sessions are observed; it is not an enjoyment, retention, or causal measure. The existing event store configures a 90-day TTL in source; live production TTL and App Store privacy labels remain unverified and must be reconciled before deployment.

After release, collect a baseline before asserting success:
- Daily puzzle starts → confirmed finishes, including solved/revealed and attempt distribution.
- Return play on subsequent days and weekly active days, counting confirmed activity rather than notification opens alone.
- Plus product-page exposure → verified purchase; cancellation, pending approval and store unavailability kept distinct.
- Refunds/restores/support reports and per-active-player backend/AI cost alongside revenue.
- Qualitative beta feedback: clarity of first clue, enjoyment after wrong guesses, satisfaction at the reveal, value of Plus.

Use existing first-party gameplay records and Apple analytics where possible. Any new event collection must be specified with minimum necessary fields and reconciled with the privacy manifest/policy before deployment. Do not collect guesses, private notes or notification contents merely to measure conversion.

Run one meaningful product or offer change at a time, keep a defined baseline/cohort, and report uncertainty when samples are small. Do not declare a winner from a single enthusiastic beta tester, clicks without purchases, or more notification delivery without more enjoyable play.
