# Rebuzzle — Small puzzle. Big aha.

## Positioning

For people who enjoy a quick daily puzzle, Rebuzzle turns pictures, words and patterns into one satisfying connection. You get three guesses, a little guidance from Eve, and a result worth sending to a friend.

Lead with the puzzle and the moment it clicks. Show real gameplay before explaining features. Use the actual app as the visual, Thorbis's calm surfaces and Inter typography, and one claim per screenshot. Avoid promising limitless play, synced XP, competitive rankings, live freeform chat, or an already-public App Store release.

## App Store metadata — prepared for the release draft

Name: Rebuzzle: Daily Word Puzzles
Subtitle: Find your daily aha
Promotional text: Small puzzle. Big aha. Find the hidden connection in three guesses, get a nudge from Eve, and turn your daily play into a collection of little wins.
Keywords: rebus,brain,teaser,word,riddle,daily,logic,picture,puzzle,challenge

Description:

A light bulb. A house. Then it clicks.

Rebuzzle is a daily puzzle about finding the connection hiding in plain sight. Pictures, words and patterns lead to one word or phrase. You have three guesses to find it.

YOUR DAILY AHA
A fresh puzzle each day. Pick it up, look a little closer, and enjoy the moment the answer makes sense.

EVE’S IN YOUR CORNER
Every guess gets clear feedback. Eve adds a short AI-powered reaction, and optional hints help you find a new angle. Your conversation stays with the result.

LITTLE WINS ADD UP
Earn XP for finishing, collect badges, and complete puzzles on three days to meet your weekly challenge. Journey progress is saved on your device. Every completed puzzle counts, including the tricky ones where you reveal the answer.

MAKE IT A FRIENDLY CHALLENGE
Send a spoiler-free result and invite someone to try the same puzzle. Your answer stays a secret.

MADE FOR YOUR IPHONE AND IPAD
Native controls, light and dark appearances, adjustable text and optional daily reminders make it easy to find your own rhythm.

Daily play is free. Optional Rebuzzle Plus adds personal journal features and deeper insights. It never adds guesses or buys an advantage.

Small puzzle. Big aha. See what clicks today.
