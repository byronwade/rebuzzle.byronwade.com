## Daily growth funnel follow-up — October 27–28, 2026

The daily funnel is implemented as first-party product interaction analytics across iOS and web. Client payloads are closed and allow only `daily_opened` with a source, `plus_offer_viewed`, `reminder_enabled`, and `daily_quality_feedback` with one of four fixed ratings. The web client reports a share-attributed daily open after session restoration, and the server records `daily_started` on the first accepted daily guess and `daily_finished` for the terminal outcome. Identity, local puzzle day, and occurrence time are derived on the server. A changed rating replaces the prior value for that account and local puzzle day. MongoDB stores only those fields plus source, outcome, or rating. The admin endpoint returns aggregates and never returns account IDs or raw event rows. Share-open → guess/finish counts are same-account, same-day overlaps; they do not establish that a distinct recipient opened or played. The four rating counts are optional, self-selected editorial input; they do not measure objective difficulty, fairness, or enjoyment.

The repository configures a 90-day TTL index and creates it before the first funnel event write. This is source-level evidence only; confirm the live production index, expiration behavior, and separate backup/log retention before stating a verified retention guarantee. Account deletion includes funnel event rows in its application transaction. The public policy source describes the code-level schedule and this verification limit; it has not been deployed or reconciled with the complete App Store privacy label.

## Native share loop follow-up — October 27, 2026

The iOS share sheet lets the player choose a destination for a result image or spoiler-safe text containing the puzzle date, result, attempt marks, hints used, and the Rebuzzle link. The chosen destination receives the content the player shares. Rebuzzle's analytics record only share-started and iOS-reported share-activity-completed event types, plus a later attributed daily open when the link returns to the app. They do not record the content, recipient, or chosen destination. An OS-reported activity completion is not proof of delivery or recipient engagement. These are source changes; the public policy and App Store privacy answers have not been published or reconciled with production.
